About Diego Olmos (DolmosMusic)

I have been involved in music production for over 6 years and have always wanted to make people truly feel the music the way i do. I create music for video games, short films and cinematic projects, and I've also worked in the music industry producing trap/rap songs as a music producer and sample maker.

Based in Murcia, Spain, I work remotely with developers, filmmakers and studios anywhere in the world to build an original soundtrack that fits the tone and pace of their project.
