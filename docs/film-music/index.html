Original music for short films

I compose original scores for short films, from €50, with an exclusive licence set out in writing. My film work so far is Cuatro Cruces, a short by Metrópolis C.E. (2024) with an orchestral, western score, plus personal soundtrack pieces published on my YouTube channel.

Who this is for

Directors and producers of short films with a production budget: projects with funding or grants, production companies and film schools. You can get in touch with a finished cut or earlier, from the script.

I work remotely from Murcia, Spain, and accept commissions from any country.

Working with picture: what I propose

This is the usual way to work on the music for a short, and how I propose to organise the commission; the details are agreed for each film:

  • What I need to start

    The cut, or the script if the film hasn't been shot yet, and references for the tone. If the cut has temp music, it helps as a guide.

  • Deciding where the music goes

    Before composing, we agree which scenes have music and what each piece has to convey.

  • Reviewing on the picture

    The usual approach is to review the music against the cut; changes are made within the revision rounds agreed in the quote.

Price, deadline and licence

All three are set in writing in the quote before work starts.

  • Price

    Commissions from €50. The final price depends on the minutes of music and the number of cues.

  • Deadline

    Set in the quote according to the scope. Tell me the date you need the music by (picture lock, a festival submission, the premiere) and I'll plan the work around it.

  • Exclusive licence in writing

    The music for your short is only for your short. The licence states the uses (festivals, distribution, online), the territory and the duration, so you have a document to show if you're asked about the music rights. I reserve the right to show the music in my portfolio from the premiere.

Frequently asked questions

Do you compose for short films too, not just video games?

Yes. I composed the original score for Cuatro Cruces, a short film by Metrópolis C.E. (2024), orchestral with a western character. Orchestral is my favourite style, in the vein of Game of Thrones, but I write in whatever style the film needs.

When should I contact a composer for my short?

As soon as you have a cut, even a rough one, or earlier with the script if you want to plan the music from the start. The earlier you write, the more room there is in the schedule for revisions before your deadline.

Can I submit the short to festivals with this music?

Yes, if the licence includes it: it covers the uses we agree in writing. Tell me where you plan to show the film (festivals, distribution, online) and those uses go in the quote.
