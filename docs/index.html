Frequently asked questions

Do you use AI to create your music?

No. I compose and produce every track myself, without generative AI. If you want to see how a track was made, ask me and I'll send you examples of the creative process.

Do you compose music for indie and small game studios?

Yes. I work with small studios, indie developers and solo creators. Most of my projects so far have been indie games: three for Betaking Studios (Duelite, Rabbit Trail and Catch & Defense), each in a different style.

How much does the music for a game cost?

Commissions start at €50 for a small project. The final price depends on the minutes of music, the number of tracks and whether sound effects are included. I send a written quote for each project, and the pricing guide explains the criteria in detail.

What kind of music do you compose?

It depends on the game: orchestral and western (Duelite), retro and chiptune (Rabbit Trail), and other styles in my personal pieces. My favourite is orchestral, in the vein of Game of Thrones.

What is the process, and how do revisions work?

We start with a conversation about the game so I understand what you need. Then I send a written quote with the tracks, the licence, the deadline and the revision rounds. I compose, you review each track, and I adjust it within those rounds until it fits the game.

Do you compose for short films too, not just video games?

Yes. I composed the original score for Cuatro Cruces, a short film by Metrópolis C.E. (2024), orchestral with a western character. Orchestral is my favourite style, in the vein of Game of Thrones, but I write in whatever style the film needs.
