Why clear project descriptions are crucial for accurate pricing

For a fair and accurate price, providing a clear and detailed project description is key. Whether you're looking for custom soundtracks or background music for your project, knowing the scope, needs, and creative direction allows us to offer the best value without surprises.

We understand that developers and small businesses often work within tight budgets. That's why we offer affordable, high-quality music solutions tailored to your needs.

  • Basic project

    Perfect for simple, short soundtracks and background music for smaller projects or videos.

  • Intermediate project

    For projects requiring custom compositions, more complex soundtracks, and music tailored to your content.

  • Advanced project

Frequently asked questions

Do you use AI to create your music?

No. I don't use AI to create my songs. If you need it, I can send you examples of the creative process.

Do you compose music for indie and small game studios?

Yes, I work with small studios, indie developers and solo creators. Most of my projects so far have been for indie games.

How much does a project usually cost?

Pricing is adaptive: not every project or budget is the same. Tell me the details of your project and I'll give you a price that fits what you need, whether it's a small short film or something bigger.

What kind of music do you compose for video games?

It depends on the project: from orchestral and western-style soundtracks to retro chiptune styles, always adapted to the tone and mechanics of your game.

What's the working process like?

We start with a conversation about your project so I understand what you need, I give you a quote based on that, and we work through revisions until the music fits your game, short film or movie perfectly.

Do you also work on short films and movies, not just video games?

Yes. Video game soundtracks are my passion, but I also compose for short films, movies and corporate videos.
