About me: Diego Olmos

I'm Diego Olmos, and DolmosMusic is the name I compose under. I write soundtracks for video games and short films from Murcia, Spain, and accept commissions from any country. I've been composing and producing music for over six years, without generative AI.

From trap production to soundtracks

Before writing soundtracks I worked as a trap and rap producer and sample maker. From there I moved on to music for games and film:

  • Trap and rap production

    I produced trap and rap beats and made samples. Part of that work is on my beats channel, linked below.

  • First soundtrack pieces, 2021–2022

    Personal pieces in different styles (traditional Japanese, indie, dark fantasy), published on YouTube and listed on the projects page.

  • Commissions for Betaking Studios and Metrópolis C.E.

    Duelite (Steam, 2024) was my first video game soundtrack, followed by Rabbit Trail (2025) and Catch & Defense (2026), all three for Betaking Studios. For film, the short Cuatro Cruces (Metrópolis C.E., 2024).

How I work

Each commission starts with a conversation about the project, so I understand what you need before quoting. Then I send a written quote with the tracks, the licence, the deadline and the revision rounds.

For games I write the music by function: each track belongs to a screen or a moment (map, battle, boss, shop), as the Duelite track list shows. I can also take on the full sound design: sound effects, foley and the rest of the game's sounds.

Composed without generative AI

I don't use generative AI to compose. Every track on this site is composed and produced by me.

If you want to check, I can send you examples of the creative process behind a track. Ask for them through the form.
